Crop Mood EOS M slim - FIXED v3

1) RAW LiveView toggle removed
   - Tapping Pic Quality / RAW on the LV bar no longer switches to H.264.
   - raw_video_touch_hit() is a no-op (Crop Mood stays RAW-only).

2) Playback navigation (video / PLAY)
   Root cause: EOS M LV transition guard (eosm_lv_guard_busy) stayed busy when
   leaving LiveView while the mode dial was still on Movie + Crop Mood on.
   gui-common then blocked ALL touch because transition_busy was true,
   including Canon PLAY and mlv_play navigation.

   Fixes:
   - crop_rec: clear the LV guard when !lv (PLAY/QR/still UI).
   - gui-common: only block touch during transition while still in LiveView.

Also includes FIXED v2 (WB photo touch, no slim_h264_mode, single Up/Down settings).
